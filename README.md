# SalesPulse – Sales Performance Analysis (Data Analyst Project)

**Tools:** Python (pandas, matplotlib) · HTML/CSS/JS dashboard (included) · CSV
**Dataset:** 240 fictional transactions, Jan–Dec 2025 (10 products, 5 categories, 5 regions, 67 customers)

## 1. Business problem
A retail/office-products business wants to know: *Where does revenue and profit come from, which products/regions/periods under-perform, and what should the sales team do next?*

## 2. Process
1. **Data prep & quality checks** – missing values, duplicates, Sales = Qty × Unit Price, negative profit, date range (all passed, 0 issues).
2. **Feature engineering** – Profit margin %, Month, Quarter, Weekday.
3. **KPI calculation** – revenue, profit, margin, orders, units, AOV, customers.
4. **EDA** – category, product, region, month/quarter, customer concentration, Pareto, region×category matrix.
5. **Visualisation & insights** – 7 charts + interactive dashboard (`dashboard/index.html`).

## 4. Key findings
1. **Revenue is concentrated in one product.** Office Chair = 47% of revenue; just 4 of 10 products give 80%. Furniture is 58% of revenue but has the *lowest* margin (20.7%).
2. **Region imbalance.** North (26.7%) and East (25.2%) lead; South is weakest (11.3%) and Central is low (15.3%) – both have half the orders (30 vs 60) of other regions. Central also has the lowest margin (19.0%).
3. **Volatile months.** April is the peak (₹2.94L) while May collapses to ₹0.87L (-70%). Order counts stay ~17–24 every month, so swings come from *big-ticket items* (chairs, monitor stands), not order volume.
4. **Margins are flat across order size** (correlation of quantity vs margin ≈ 0.01), so bulk orders are not being discounted – there's room to test volume pricing.
5. **High-margin, low-revenue items:** Desk Lamp (24.3%), Pen Pack (23.8%), Monitor Stand (23.1%) – candidates for bundling with chairs.

## 5. Recommendations
- Reduce dependence on Office Chair: bundle accessories (Monitor Stand, Lamp, Keyboard) with every chair order.
- Run targeted campaigns/channel partners in **South and Central** (they have only 30 orders each).
- Plan inventory and promotions around the Apr/May swing; investigate why May dropped.
- Review Furniture pricing/cost – largest category with lowest margin; +1pt margin ≈ ₹11.5k extra profit.

## 6. Limitations
- Data is synthetic and generated by a formula, so patterns (e.g. every customer being a repeat buyer, exactly 60/30 orders per region) are artefacts; real data will be messier.
- One year only – no year-over-year comparison or forecasting.
- Currency shown in INR; no conversion applied.
